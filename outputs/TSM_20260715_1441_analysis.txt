# TSMC (TSM) — Value Analysis

## 1. What the Three Scenarios Tell Us

The bear, base, and bull cases present a remarkably consistent picture: **TSMC appears deeply undervalued at $420 across every scenario tested.**

| Scenario | Intrinsic Value | Price/IV Ratio | Zone |
|----------|----------------|----------------|------|
| Bear | $4,217 | 0.10 (10¢/$1) | IDEAL |
| Base | $11,723 | 0.04 (4¢/$1) | IDEAL |
| Bull | $19,870 | 0.02 (2¢/$1) | IDEAL |

Even the **most conservative scenario** — which applies a 12% discount rate specifically to account for geopolitical risk and assumes mean reversion away from TSMC's 18.9% revenue CAGR [1] — produces an intrinsic value roughly **10x the current price**. The base case, anchored directly to the knowledge base's documented parameters of g1=20%, g2=12%, terminal=4%, dr=11% [4], suggests the stock trades at roughly **4 cents on the dollar**.

The 11% discount rate used in the base case is already described as the **highest in the portfolio**, explicitly baking in geopolitical risk as a known concern [4]. The bear case adds another turn of the screw on top of that. The fact that even this ultra-conservative scenario still shows overwhelming undervaluation is significant.

TSMC's underlying business metrics support this picture: **ROE of 36.2%, 5-year FCF CAGR of 24%, and revenue CAGR of 18.9%** are the numbers of a compounding machine, not a commodity manufacturer.

---

## 2. Would Buffett Buy, Hold, or Avoid?

**Buffett would buy — and buy aggressively.**

The margin-of-safety framework is unambiguous here. The ideal buy zone is defined as paying **≤70 cents per $1 of intrinsic value** [2]. At a bear-case ratio of 10 cents, TSMC is trading at a fraction of even that threshold. Margin of safety means protecting yourself from being wrong — if you think a bridge holds 30 tons, only drive 10-ton trucks across it [1]. Here, the "bridge" holds 10 tons in the worst case, and you're driving a 1-ton truck. The protection is extraordinary.

The "arms dealer" framing matters here [4]: TSMC profits regardless of *which* chip designer wins the AI race — Nvidia, AMD, Apple, Broadcom, Google, or Cerebras all flow through TSMC's fabs. This is precisely the kind of **durable, widening moat** that Buffett prizes above almost everything else [5].

**Verdict: Strong buy at $420.**

---

## 3. What Would Need to Change to Enter Buy Territory?

**Nothing — it is already deep inside buy territory.** The more relevant question is what would need to change to make this *less* attractive or push it toward hold/avoid:

- **Geopolitical escalation** over Taiwan that materially impairs TSMC's ability to operate or raises the required discount rate well beyond 12%
- **Technological disruption** that breaks the manufacturing moat — a competitor reaching 2nm-class production at scale
- **A dramatic price re-rating** — the stock would need to roughly **6x from current prices just to reach the bear-case IV**, and roughly **28x to reach the base-case IV**
- **A significant deterioration in FCF margins** that invalidates the 24% CAGR as a forward anchor

Until one of those occurs, the framework indicates this stock remains in the ideal accumulation zone [2].
