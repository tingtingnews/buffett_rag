# Reddit, Inc. (RDDT) — Value Analysis

## 1. What the Three Scenarios Tell Us

The scenario spread is wide and deeply instructive. At $96:

| Scenario | Intrinsic Value | Price/IV | Zone |
|----------|----------------|----------|------|
| Bear | $26 | 3.75x | Trim |
| Base | $51 | 1.89x | Trim |
| Bull | $103 | 0.93x | Accumulate Cautiously |

**The market is essentially pricing in the bull case as the base case.** Only under the most optimistic assumptions — where Reddit's network effects [3] continue compounding and near-historical growth rates persist — does the stock approach fair value at a 0.93x ratio. Even then, that only reaches "Accumulate Cautiously" territory, not a genuine buy signal.

The 217% FCF CAGR is real but dangerously misleading as a forward input. It is almost certainly a low-base, early-monetization artifact. The bear case is right to flag mean-reversion risk explicitly [1].

---

## 2. Would Buffett Buy, Hold, or Avoid?

**Avoid at current price. Almost certainly.**

Here is the reasoning, card by card:

**On margin of safety [1][2]:** The bridge analogy is clarifying here. Even if you believe Reddit's 32% ROE and revenue momentum are durable, you are paying $96 for something worth $51 in a reasonable base case. That is not buying at a discount — that is paying *nearly twice intrinsic value*. Card [2] is unambiguous: a price-to-IV ratio above 1.00 means do not buy. At 1.89x in the base case, the stock sits far outside every buy zone in the cents-per-dollar framework.

**On intrinsic value [5]:** Intrinsic value is a range, not a point. When *all three* scenarios — bear, base, and bull — cluster between $26 and $103, the honest takeaway is that uncertainty is enormous. Card [5] warns that two analysts with the same facts will reach different values. Here, the range is nearly 4:1 from bottom to top. That spread itself is a risk signal, not a reason to anchor to the high end.

**On the moat [3][4]:** Reddit's network effects and user-generated content corpus are real moat sources. The community lock-in and unique data corpus (valuable for AI licensing) are legitimate competitive advantages. Buffett would respect this. But a real moat at the wrong price is still a bad investment. Buffett has explicitly said he would rather buy a wonderful company at a fair price than a fair company at a wonderful price — but $96 against a base IV of $51 is not a fair price for even a wonderful business.

---

## 3. What Would Need to Change for a Buy Signal

Per card [2], the ideal entry zone is ≤70¢ per $1 of intrinsic value. Working backward from the base case IV of $51:

> **Target buy price ≤ $36 (70¢ × $51)**
> Acceptable entry ≤ $41 (80¢ × $51)

For the stock to enter buy territory without a price decline, the base case IV itself would need to rise materially — which would require:

1. **Sustained revenue growth** validating the 48.9% CAGR as durable, not cyclical
2. **FCF conversion maturing** — the 217% FCF CAGR normalizing at a still-high but credible level
3. **Demonstrated moat widening** [3] — evidence that ad pricing power and data licensing revenues are compounding, not one-time
4. **Reduced execution risk** — a longer track record of profitability that justifies compressing the discount rate used in the bear case [1]

None of these are impossible. Reddit is a genuinely interesting business. But at today's price, the market has already priced in the optimistic version of that future.
